Beginner's guide to Football


Basic Rules to follow
● Maximum of 5 Subs per game.
● Minimum 11 Players on the pitch, for each team.
● Hand-balls are strictly restricted, excluding the GK.
● If in an offside postion during the play, the goal is considered unvalid.
Required Equipment/s
● Football Boots or Cleats.
● Water Bottle.
● Goal-Net.
● Court.
● Proper team attire;
-Jersey
-Shorts
-Cleats
Basic Skills
● No-Look Pass.
● Step overs.
● Rainbow Flicks.
● Slide Tackling.
● Trivela.
● Nutmeg.
